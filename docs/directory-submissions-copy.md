**Product Name:** CosmetCheck

**Tagline / Short Description:**
Compliance platform for cosmetics brands selling to Brazil and Mexico. Check ingredient lists against ANVISA and COFEPRIS regulations in seconds.

**Full Description:**
CosmetCheck is a compliance automation platform designed for cosmetics brands, cross-border ecommerce sellers, and manufacturers who sell or plan to sell to Brazil and Mexico — the two largest beauty markets in Latin America.

Brazil's ANVISA and Mexico's COFEPRIS maintain strict cosmetics regulations that differ significantly from EU and US standards. A single banned ingredient can result in customs rejection, inventory destruction, and thousands of dollars in losses. CosmetCheck eliminates this risk by providing instant ingredient compliance checks.

**Key Features:**
- Ingredient Compliance Checker: Paste your INCI ingredient list and get instant status for each ingredient — Banned, Restricted, or Allowed — for both Brazil (ANVISA RDC 907/2024) and Mexico (COFEPRIS NOM-141-SSA1)
- 2,000+ Ingredient Database: Continuously updated database mapping regulated substances, concentration limits, and regulatory citations
- AI Listing Generator: Automatically generates compliant product listings in Portuguese and Spanish with proper regulatory disclaimers
- Batch CSV Detection: Upload CSV files to check up to 100 products at once (Pro tier)
- Regulatory Alerts: Get notified when Brazil or Mexico updates their cosmetics regulations
- Country Overview Pages: Dedicated pages for Brazil and Mexico compliance requirements
- Ingredient Database: Browse 50+ commonly regulated ingredients with detailed health risk profiles and compliant alternatives

**Use Cases:**
- Cross-border ecommerce sellers expanding to Amazon Brazil, Mercado Livre, or Mercado Libre Mexico
- Cosmetics brands exporting to Latin America
- Private label manufacturers verifying formula compliance for LATAM clients
- Compliance consultants conducting rapid ingredient screening
- Amazon FBA sellers preventing customs rejection and inventory loss

**Pricing:**
- Free Tier: 10 compliance checks per month, single-country checks, basic ingredient database access
- Pro Tier: $29/month — unlimited checks, Brazil + Mexico combined checks, AI listing generation in Portuguese and Spanish, batch CSV detection, regulatory alerts, priority support

**Headquarters:** Remote-first
