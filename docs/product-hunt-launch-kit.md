## 二、Maker 第一条评论（Launch Comment）

```
Hi Product Hunters! 👋

I'm Stormy, founder of CosmetCheck. Here's why we built this:

Last year, a friend lost $40K of skincare inventory because Brazil's ANVISA rejected her shipment over a banned preservative. She had no idea. The ingredient was legal in the US and EU.

That's the problem: cosmetics regulations vary wildly by country, and Latin America (Brazil + Mexico = 350M consumers) has some of the strictest rules on the planet. But no tool existed to check ingredient compliance specifically for these markets.

So we built CosmetCheck:

✅ Paste your ingredient list → instant ban/restriction status for Brazil & Mexico
✅ 2,000+ regulated ingredients mapped to ANVISA RDC 907/2024 and COFEPRIS NOM-141-SSA1
✅ AI generates compliant product listings in Portuguese & Spanish
✅ 10 free checks, no credit card

We'd love your feedback! What's the most painful compliance issue you've faced when selling internationally?

Free tier: https://cosmetcheck.com
```
