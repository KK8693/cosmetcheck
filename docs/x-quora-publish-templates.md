**步骤：**
1. 访问 https://www.quora.com/What-are-the-most-common-reasons-cosmetics-are-rejected-by-ANVISA
2. 点击 "Answer"
3. 粘贴以下内容

**回答内容：**
```
After working with 50+ brands shipping to Brazil, here are the top rejection reasons:

1. Claims Classification (40% of rejections)
Your "anti-aging serum" claims to "reduce wrinkles" → ANVISA says it's a drug, not a cosmetic. Cosmetics CANNOT claim therapeutic effects.

2. Banned Ingredients (25%)
Hydroquinone, mercury compounds, lead in lip/eye products — these are outright banned. Even trace amounts trigger rejection.

3. Missing Portuguese Labels (15%)
All text on packaging must be in Portuguese. English-only labels = automatic rejection.

4. Incomplete Documentation (12%)
Missing batch numbers, no expiration dates, absent manufacturer info — ANVISA checks every detail.

5. Concentration Violations (8%)
Restricted ingredients (salicylic acid, benzoyl peroxide) have maximum allowed concentrations.

The frustrating part? Many of these are preventable with a pre-shipment check. I now run every product through a compliance scanner before shipping. Costs nothing for the first 10 products.

The $200 I spent on rejected inventory last year? Zero this year. Prevention is cheaper than customs storage fees.
```
